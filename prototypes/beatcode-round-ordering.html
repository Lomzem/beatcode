Decision captured by this artifact

Use authoritative admission order, immutable restriction snapshots, private later passing verdicts, and a closed settlement phase. Award the lowest passing sequence only after every earlier eligible job is final. A 90-second ceiling that leaves a possible earlier winner unresolved voids the round; it never promotes a faster queue result. Recovery reconstructs effects from round authority and keeps editing state intact.

Policy tensions: closing admission when any job passes is itself an indirect correctness signal even when the verdict stays private. Neutral “settling” language limits detail but cannot eliminate that signal. The editor decision allows challenge-gated sample runs, while the card contract says samples remain usable; this artifact follows the more specific card contract. Strict compiler flags remain an unresolved architecture contract: a strict verdict here is manually supplied.

Scope: one round, not a match implementation. Replacement/abort policy, identity authorization, real compilation, local source caching and disconnect forfeits are not simulated. “Recover” reconstructs a view from retained authority in this tab; it does not survive a real page reload. No network, storage, editor library, tests, or production integration.
